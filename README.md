# Dress to Impress — brat runway (fan-made)

A browser dress-up game inspired by Roblox's *Dress to Impress*, with a **2025 brat collection / brat runway** event.

## Play
Double-click `index.html` (no install needed), or serve the folder:

```bash
python -m http.server 8642 --directory dress-to-impress-brat
```

Progress saves automatically in your browser.

## Features
- **Classic Runway**: a random theme (24 of them), a dressing timer, and 7 AI rivals. Everyone walks the runway, you rate their looks 1–5★, and the judges score yours.
- **Dressing room**: 200+ pieces across hair, face, makeup, tops, bottoms, dresses, jackets, shoes, legwear, hats, glasses, jewelry, bags/props, gloves and wings. Each piece has custom colors and 14 patterns. Also skin tones, height, poses, undo, random, reset and 6 saved-outfit slots.
- **Runway**: walking animation, camera flashes, crowd reactions, and live posing with keys 1–8 on your turn.
- **Progression**: coins, XP ranks from Fashion Newbie to Top Model, a shop for locked items, a VIP pass (VIP items and 2× coins) and redeemable codes (`WELCOME`, `IMPRESS`, `RUNWAY`, `SLAY`, `BRATSUMMER`, `LIMEGREEN`, `CLUBCLASSICS`).
- **brat runway event**: a lime runway with strobes and a club beat, 8 brat themes, a 20-piece brat collection you unlock with 💚 tokens, brat quests, and a brat cover maker that saves a PNG.
- **Wardrobe**: free play with no timer, plus a photo booth with backgrounds, poses and PNG export.
- All music and sound effects are synthesized live with WebAudio, so there are no audio files.
