# Dress to Impress — seasons (fan-made)

A browser dress-up game inspired by Roblox's *Dress to Impress*.

**Play:** https://yuvikavaidya-cpu.github.io/dress-to-impress-brat/

## Play locally
Double-click `index.html`, or serve the folder:

```bash
python -m http.server 8642 --directory dress-to-impress-brat
```

Progress saves automatically in your browser.

## Features
- **Classic Runway**: 36 themes, including Pool Party, Swim Team, Wedding Guest, Here Comes the Bride, Christmas, New Year's Eve, Easter, Lunar New Year, Diwali, St. Patrick's Day, Autumn and Baddie. You dress against a timer alongside 7 AI rivals, rate each other on the runway, and get judged.
- **Seasons**: each season has its own stage, music, quests and unlockable collection.
  - 🎃 **Spooky Season** (Halloween): candy currency, vampire capes, skeleton suits, a pumpkin dress and more.
  - 🪔 **Navaratri Utsav**: diya currency, chaniya choli, lehenga, saree, dandiya sticks, jhumkas and more.
  - 💚 **brat runway**: the 2025 brat collection and a brat cover maker.
- 🇮🇳 **Festivals of India**: 15 festivals, including Holi, Diwali, Raksha Bandhan, Eid, Makar Sankranti and Pongal, Onam, Ganesh Chaturthi, Karva Chauth, Lohri, Baisakhi, Janmashtami, Bihu, Teej, Independence Day, and Ugadi and Gudi Padwa. There are 37 festive pieces, such as the sharara, hijab, kasavu saree, nauvari, anarkali, sherwani, phulkari, patiala, pichkari, kite and dhol. You earn 🪔 diyas.
- **Pop updates** (fan-inspired, with no real likenesses):
  - 😼 **KATSEYE**: dance-practice and debut-stage looks, earning 🐾 paw prints.
  - 💜 **Olivia Rodrigo**: purple GUTS and SOUR grunge-pop looks, earning 🦋 butterflies.
  - 🎤 **K-Pop Idols**: 16 group-inspired concepts (BLACKPINK, NewJeans, aespa, TWICE, LE SSERAFIM, IVE, ITZY, ILLIT, Stray Kids, BTS, SEVENTEEN, BABYMONSTER, Red Velvet, airport fashion, award shows and comeback stages), earning 💫 stars.
- **45 hairstyles**, including hime cut, butterfly layers, box braids, locs, hush cut, split dye, ombre and two-block.
- **More realistic rendering**: hair strand texture, iris gradients, eyelid creases and a sculpted nose.
- 💅 **Baddie Pass, Season 1**: 15 levels with free and premium reward tracks.
- **Characters**: soft 3D shading, 390+ pieces, 22 patterns, custom colors, poses and a photo booth.
- **Progression**: coins, ranks, a VIP pass and codes (`WELCOME`, `SPOOKY`, `GARBA`, `BADDIE`, `BRATSUMMER`, and more).
- All music and sound effects are synthesized live with WebAudio, so there are no audio files.
